Key Concepts & Intuition Guide

Padding ($P$)

Adds border pixels (typically zeros) around the input. Why? Prevents spatial dimensions from shrinking rapidly across deep layers and allows border edge pixels to contribute equally to features.

Stride ($S$)

The step size of the kernel movement. $S > 1$ downsamples the spatial size of the feature map without max pooling, skipping adjacent pixel computations.

Dilation ($D$)

Expands the kernel's coverage by inserting spaces between weights (Atrous Conv). Increases receptive field significantly without increasing parameter count or compute weights!
